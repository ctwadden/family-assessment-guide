UNDERSTANDING ASSESSMENT TYPES IN POWERSCHOOL

Three ways to see learning.

At Halifax West, teachers gather evidence of student learning in a variety of ways. The following codes appear at the beginning of assessment titles to help students and families understand how learning is being assessed.

P ProductTests, assignments, projects, presentations, or other completed work.

C ConversationDiscussions, conferences, interviews, oral explanations, or student reflections.

O ObservationSkills, processes, and demonstrations of learning observed by the teacher.

Together, products, conversations, and observations provide a more complete picture of student achievement and growth. The learning goals and criteria explain what is being assessed. The letters do not assign equal weights or require three marks for every task.

FORM · Formative
Feedback during learning. In this course convention, these practice entries are excluded from the grade calculation.
SUM · Summative
Evidence used to evaluate achievement against learning criteria. It can come from P, C, or O, and may include further opportunities to demonstrate learning.

In the documented Nova Scotia parent portal, the Category column appears beside the assessment. A category such as Graphic Design groups the learning area. The title identifies the evidence and its purpose. Read the description and feedback for the learning goal, what was demonstrated, and the next step.

To find it in the web portal, open In-Progress Achievement and Attendance, then choose Assessment under View Details By for the course. Nova Scotia’s Parent Portal guide, page 5, documents this view. Screens may differ by version and school configuration.
